Food Service Hot Water

Food-Service Hot Water Systems Built for the Rush

In a restaurant, the dish machine, the sinks, and the handwashing stations all pull from the same supply, and an undersized or failing heater shows up first as lukewarm water at the dish machine and a backed-up dish room. A high-temp dish machine typically needs final rinse water near 180°F, which almost always calls for a booster heater paired with the main supply. Our food-service work covers high-input tanks, tankless racks, booster heaters, and peak-demand sizing, and we schedule every visit around your prep and service times.

Temperature is where many restaurant hot water problems start. If the main heater can't keep up, handwashing sinks go lukewarm, the three-compartment sink can't hold its wash temperature, and the dish machine struggles to sanitize at peak service. We verify temperatures from the tank all the way to the fixtures and document every visit in written reports your inspector and landlord can review.

Your schedule comes first. Our team coordinates every replacement and maintenance visit with owners, managers, and kitchen staff. Off-hours installation is available when needed, and we keep downtime to a minimum so the kitchen opens on time.

Code & Inspections

Code-Compliant Installation and Documentation

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in tanks stored too cool, and costly water damage when a tank fails. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, and each visit ends with a written report listing risks, priorities, and recommended preventive steps.

Consistent professional service protects your people and your property, and acting on expert recommendations keeps small issues from becoming liabilities. It also creates a record. We keep service logs, temperature records, and a binder of reports for every account, and we increase visit frequency for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and central plant rooms. When a landlord, insurer, or inspector asks for proof of due diligence, it's ready.

Emergency Service

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Scranton Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties across Scranton and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

Commercial emergencies come in many forms. The cause could be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor fault, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank throwing error codes. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.
