How to Tell Something Is Wrong

Water heaters usually give plenty of warning before they fail, as long as you know what to look and listen for. Here are the most common indicators that it's time to call a professional:

  • Rust flaking from the tank jacket or its connections
  • A tank at or past the eight-to-twelve-year mark
  • Hot water running out faster every month
  • Repeated service calls on the same aging heater
  • Banging and popping from a tank packed with sediment
  • Energy bills rising while hot water gets harder to come by
  • Rusty or brown water from the hot side only
  • A slow leak or damp spot under the tank itself

Why Hire a Professional for Water Heater Replacement?

Consider the vent first. A gas water heater's exhaust contains carbon monoxide, and a vent that is undersized, poorly pitched, or loosely connected can let it spill back into your home. Add a gas connection that has to be leak-free, an electrical circuit that has to match the new heater's load, and a relief valve that has to carry the right rating with a discharge line that is never capped or undersized, and the details pile up quickly. Many homes also need an expansion tank, a drain pan, or seismic straps under local code, and some jurisdictions require a permit. A move to tankless or a heat pump brings gas line sizing, new vent materials, circuits, and condensate lines into the job.

Water Heater Care Tips From Our Technicians

A few simple habits, like an annual flush and a quick look at the relief valve, can add years to a tank. Here are our recommendations:

  • Tankless units need a yearly descaling, especially where the water is hard
  • Avoid running the tank hotter than about 120 degrees unless there is a specific need
  • Plan ahead: knowing the heater's age lets you replace it on your schedule, not after a leak
  • Heat pump units need a clean filter and open airflow to run efficiently
  • Keep household water pressure in a safe range to protect the tank and its fittings
  • Have the anode rod checked around year three to five and replaced if it is worn
  • Start a flushing routine in the first year, before sediment hardens
